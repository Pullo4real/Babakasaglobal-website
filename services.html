Services for mineral buyers

What we do for buyers

From the first enquiry to cargo on the water, one team handles the work.

Mineral sourcing

We source gold, gemstones and industrial minerals from Nigerian mining regions to match your grade and quantity.

Quality and inspection

We coordinate independent inspection of quality and weight by a recognised inspector before shipment.

Contracts and paperwork

Offer, sales agreement, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and the export permits each commodity needs.

Sea freight

Containers and bulk through Nigerian seaports, on FOB, CIF, CFR or EXW terms.

Air freight and courier

High-value parcels by air through DHL, FedEx, UPS and other licensed carriers from Lagos and Abuja.

Custom sourcing

Looking for a mineral that is not on our list? Tell us the specification and we will look for it.

Common questions
What do you supply?

Gold, gemstones and industrial minerals from Nigeria, including lithium ore, coal, charcoal, kaolin, fluorspar, barite and more. See the full list on the Our Minerals page, or ask us for a mineral that is not listed.

Do you publish prices?

No. Pricing depends on the mineral, grade, quantity, packaging and destination, so we quote each request individually. Send a request and we reply with terms.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form on the Contact page. Choose your commodity and the form asks the right questions for it. You can also message us on WhatsApp.

How fast will you reply?

Our export desk replies within 24 to 48 hours, by email or WhatsApp.

Do you sell to buyers inside Nigeria?

Yes. See the "Clients in Nigeria" page for what we need from you and how collection and delivery work.

Can you export to my country?

Yes. We ship by sea and by air to buyers in Europe, the Gulf, Asia, the Americas and beyond. The "Clients outside Nigeria" page explains documents, inspection, incoterms, seaports and couriers.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

FOB, CIF, CFR and EXW, depending on what you need. Terms are agreed in the contract.

Can I inspect before shipment?

Yes. Independent inspection by a recognised inspector such as SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek can be arranged before cargo ships.

How do I know you are registered?

Babakasa Global Investment Ltd is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria, RC 1974668. You can check the number on the CAC public search.

Where are you located?

Our head office is at No. 20 Yola Bye-pass, Sabon Pegi, Yola South, Adamawa State, Nigeria.
